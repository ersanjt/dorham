# 00 — North star

## What Dorham is

**Dorham (دورهم)** is the gathering place for Iranians living in Turkey.

People open it to see what is happening in their city this week, to go to a public event, and to meet verified humans. Dating is one tab, added after events work.

## What Dorham is not

- Not Tinder, Sheytoon, or Azizam with a new skin
- Not a hookup grid
- Not a product for users inside Iran in v1
- Not a content network that needs influencers to survive

## One-sentence pitch

دورهم جایی است که ایرانی‌های یک شهر — اول استانبول — دور هم جمع می‌شوند، نه جایی که بی‌نهایت کارت سوایپ می‌کنند.

## Success in 12 months

1. A weekly Istanbul event that 30+ people actually attend
2. Women stay, because profiles are verified and the feed is not a male swarm
3. Revenue from tickets + verification, not from locking swipes
4. A codebase another engineer can extend without guessing the product

## Decision filter

If a feature does not help someone **meet a real person in this city**, it waits.
