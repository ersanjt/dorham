# 01 — Business plan (locked)

## Problem

Iranians in Turkey are numerous and isolated. Telegram groups are noisy, Instagram is not a city, and Persian dating apps are swipe paywalls with fake proximity. People need a **local, verified, IRL reason to leave the house**.

## Customer

**Primary:** Iranian women and men, 22–40, living in Istanbul (residence or long stay), Persian + English.
**Secondary:** second-generation / mixed couples who want Persian-speaking community.
**Not v1:** users physically in Iran (payments, sanctions, store, risk).

## Offer

1. City events (hosted + community)
2. Verified profiles
3. Optional dating tab only after events have liquidity

## Why we can win (founder fit)

The founder is Iranian and lives in Turkey. Sheytoon is LA. Azizam is Cologne. Neither can seed Istanbul in person every week. That is the moat in year one — not the algorithm.

## Revenue (in this order)

| Stream | When | Price (direction) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event tickets | Month 1 | 150–400 TRY local / €8–15 diaspora |
| Verification badge | Month 2 | €6–12 one-time or yearly |
| Host / cafe listing | Month 6 | paid placement for Iranian venues |
| Community subscription | Month 9 | extra events, city filter — **never** lock basic discovery |

Apple / Google billing for diaspora. **Until that exists, Istanbul tickets are cash at the door.** The app records who owes and who paid when they check in. Iyzico later for local tickets if store fees hurt.

## Unit economics (year 1 target)

- CAC: near zero (Telegram, Instagram, IRL flyers, founder events)
- If we buy ads before 3 successful events, we are off path
- Year-1 base case if Istanbul sticks: **$3k–$15k / month**
- That is a lifestyle business. Do not raise a Tinder story.

## Go to market

1. Founder hosts 4 Friday events in Istanbul before asking anyone to “download and swipe”
2. App is RSVP + guest list + safety, not the party itself
3. Women-first invite list for the first 3 events
4. Public web page for each event so people who do not install still come

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| No women | Verify men, cap male-only events, host with a woman co-host |
| Empty city | One city until 200 monthly actives |
| Paywall hate | Free discovery forever |
| Legal / KVKK | Privacy policy, consent, minimize GPS |
| Iran users | Explicit “Turkey-based community” copy |
| LGBTQ module | Not a Turkey-entity v1 |

## 18-month milestones

- M3: 4 events, 80 installs, verified flow live
- M6: 200 MAU Istanbul, tickets covering venue
- M12: second city only if Istanbul events are full
- M18: dating tab if women ask for it in writing (survey), not because we are bored
